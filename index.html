Frequently asked questions

What documents do I need to rent a car? +

A valid driver's licence (2+ years), personal ID or passport, and proof of residence or employment.

Is the security deposit refundable? +

Yes — deposits ($100, $300 or $500 depending on the vehicle) are refunded on safe return of the vehicle.

How much free mileage do I get? +

200km free every day, with excess mileage billed at $0.60/km.

Do you offer airport pickup? +

Yes — free pickup and handover at RGM International Airport, plus free delivery anywhere in Harare.

Can I hire a car with a driver? +

Yes — chauffeur service is available on any vehicle for an additional daily fee.
